Information We Collect Information We Do Not Collect Directly We do not collect your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information directly through the App. Information Collected Automatically The following types of data may be collected automatically through third-party services integrated in the App: • Device information – device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers • Usage data – app interactions, session duration, in-app events • Advertising identifiers – IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) on iOS, used for personalized advertising • Crash reports and diagnostics – to improve app stability Game Progress Data Game progress (coins, energy, unlocked locations, grid state) is stored locally on your device using UserDefaults. This data is not transmitted to our servers. Third-Party Services The App uses the following third-party services which may collect and process data independently: Google AdMob We use Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to display rewarded video advertisements in the App's Daily Gift feature. When you choose to watch an ad, AdMob may collect and process the following data: • Advertising Identifier (IDFA) – a unique, resettable identifier used to serve personalized ads and measure ad performance • IP address – used to determine approximate location for ad targeting and fraud prevention • Device information – device model, OS version, screen size, language and time zone settings • Ad interaction data – whether you watched, skipped, or clicked an ad • App usage signals – how you use the App, used for ad relevance and frequency capping • Coarse location data – derived from IP address (not GPS), used for regional ad targeting Personalized vs. Non-personalized ads: If you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt, AdMob may show you personalized ads based on your interests. If you decline, you will still see ads but they will not be based on your personal profile. How AdMob Uses This Data • To select and deliver relevant advertisements • To measure ad effectiveness and verify ad impressions • To prevent fraudulent activity • To build aggregate, anonymized audience insights for advertisers Your Choices Regarding AdMob • Opt out of personalized ads: Go to iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable tracking for Bruno's Forest • Reset your IDFA: Go to iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Reset Advertising Identifier • Google's ad settings: adssettings.google.com Google AdMob is operated by Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA. For more information on how Google processes data: • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy • Google AdMob Partner Guidelines: support.google.com/admob Apple App Store & iOS Apple may collect data through the App Store and iOS system services. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy at apple.com/privacy. App Tracking Transparency (ATT): In accordance with Apple's ATT framework, the App will request your permission before accessing your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized advertising. You can change your choice at any time in your iPhone Settings → Privacy → Tracking.
